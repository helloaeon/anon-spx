How anon.spx works

Your private account

Connect an Ethereum wallet and sign Tacit’s free identity message. The signature derives your Tacit account locally in this browser. The same wallet can reopen it by signing again. Only sign this message in an app you trust, and never share your wallet recovery phrase or identity signature.

Shield SPX

Choose an amount of SPX6900 and approve the exact token amount. Your browser derives the note and submits the SPX deposit to the existing Tacit V1 pool. Tacit’s relay generates an SP1 proof and settles the wrap into a spendable private note.

Send and claim

Send from a private note to a recipient’s Ethereum mainnet Tacit address (tacit1…). Your browser prepares the operation and encrypted payment memo; Tacit’s relay generates the SP1 proof and submits the transfer to V1. The payment is a one-time claimable transfer. The recipient scans for incoming payments and claims the SPX into their private balance. An unclaimed payment can be refunded by its sender after 90 days; the encrypted recovery record is kept in the sender’s browser.

Withdraw SPX

Choose a private note and an Ethereum destination. Tacit’s relay generates the SP1 proof and submits the withdrawal to V1. The destination and withdrawal amount are public on Ethereum.

Split or merge notes

In Notes, select two or more notes to merge, or choose one note to split into two. Tacit’s relay generates an SP1 proof and settles the change to your account; the relay fee is accounted for in the outputs.

Activity

The Activity popup lists incoming payments that need claiming and actions recorded in this browser session. It shows progress and transaction links when available. Open sends are checked for claims so claimed payments are removed from the refundable list.

Network and privacy

This app is configured for Ethereum mainnet and SPX6900. In the default flow, your browser derives account keys and note data and prepares each operation, but Tacit’s relay—not your device—generates the SP1 proof and submits settlement to V1. The relay receives the operation witness, including the notes an operation spends and creates and how they link within that operation; it does not receive your wallet identity or notes untouched by that operation. Deposits and withdrawals are public token transfers; the pool is designed to hide which private note funds an action, though timing and amounts at the public boundary may be correlatable.

Every wallet transaction requires your approval. Review the recipient, amount, and network in your wallet before confirming.
